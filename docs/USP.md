## The 7 USPs that define Lumin

### 1. We sell *outcomes*, not *opportunities*

Every other premium dating product sells access — more swipes, more boosts, more visibility. Hinge has 1.95M paying users at ~$340/year ARPU on a "designed to be deleted" promise it does not measure or guarantee ([Hinge Revenue and Usage Statistics, 2026](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/hinge-statistics/)).

Lumin commits to the *post-app* outcome. Every match has a structured debrief. Every cancellation has an outcome survey. Every Concierge member sees their date-conversion rate as the visible metric — not message count, not profile views. The Concierge tier carries a **"five real dates in 90 days or your next quarter is on us"** guarantee that no incumbent offers.

**Why this is defensible:** the only metric system that compounds is the one that survives the relationship. Match Group's $689M Hinge business measures swipes; we measure marriages. After ~50k labeled outcomes, our ranker is uncloneable.

Product velocity has slowed; the team is being folded into Jeevansathi. Lumin enters India with the only credible premium incumbent in maintenance mode.

The macro:
- Bumble shrinking.

- Lunch Actually still profitable on $5.7M revenue in Singapore alone after 22 years ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/lunch-actually)) — proving the matchmaking thesis but capped by human-scale economics.

The window for a tech-leveraged premium entrant is open and short.

### 4. India DPDP timing is our durable moat

[India DPDP Act final rules notified November 13, 2025](https://www.cookieyes.com/blog/india-digital-personal-data-protection-act-dpdpa/). Phased enforcement:
- Data Protection Board: live now (Nov 13, 2025).
- Consent managers: Nov 13, 2026.
- **Full compliance: May 13, 2027.**

Penalties: ₹250cr (~$30M) max per breach.

Lumin's launch window (mid-2026 SG, late-2026/early-2027 IN) lands **precisely as DPDP enforcement teeth come in**. Incumbents (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, Aisle) were built before DPDP; their architectures push EU/US-residency by default. Lumin is built with Mumbai-region data residency, per-user envelope encryption on sensitive fields, and an SDF-ready audit posture from commit #1.

This is the kind of compliance moat that is invisible until a regulator acts, then becomes the only thing that matters.

### 5. The Sparkle Doctrine — AI you can trust by *design*

EU AI Act Article 50 transparency requirements come into force **August 2026**. Every AI-touched message in any consumer product targeting EU users (and any sensible defensive global product) must be labeled.

Lumin's response is opinionated and product-led:

- **One AI affordance:** the ✦ sparkle icon, in lavender `#B8A4D9`. Used everywhere AI touches the product — suggestion chips, narrative captions, the Concierge orb. Never camouflaged.
- **AI never sends.** Composer is sacred. Suggestion chips appear above the keyboard; the human always presses send.
- **AI may quote, may not infer.** Compatibility narratives are constrained by a JSON schema that only permits facts present in either profile. Hard refusal on sexuality / religion / mental health / political views unless the user explicitly stated them.
- **One named Concierge.** Never "Assistant," never "AI." The Concierge persona is the brand. Voice is consistent: warm, literate, slightly dry.
- **No autonomous mediator in v1.** Not for product reasons but trust reasons. We will not be the company that ships an agent that talks to your match on your behalf — the failure mode is catastrophic.

Anchored to peer-reviewed honesty: [Joel & Eastwick 2020 PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1917036117) showed similarity self-report explains <1% of relationship satisfaction variance. We position our AI as a *scale lever for human curators*, not as the matchmaker. Customers feel the difference.

### 6. The Ritual — Sunday 7pm, three matches, no scroll

Every other dating product is built for infinite scroll. Lumin runs on a **ritual**. Every Sunday at 7pm local time, three curated members appear, revealed sequentially, with no scroll-back. Three actions: *Pass with thanks*, *Save for later*, *Send an opener*.

This is the Spotify-Discover-Weekly insight applied to romance: same time, same container, different content. Variable reward inside a predictable cadence creates anticipation rather than compulsion.

The ritual is **also the moat**. It is the only operating model that lets us:
- Run weekly compatibility batches end-to-end (Temporal-orchestrated).
- Give curators a finite, repeatable batch to review.
- Surface AI narratives without latency pressure.
- Charge a premium that incumbents can't credibly copy without re-architecting their whole product.

### 7. The City Strategy — density before scale

Below ~2,000 active members per metro, premium dating apps feel empty (The League's stagnation is the case study). Lumin's go-to-market is **city-by-city saturation**, not national paid acquisition:

- **Singapore first (M0–M6):** 500-member invite-only beta sourced from founder networks (ex-McKinsey/Bain/Stripe SG/Sea/Grab alumni). Three launch dinners at Atlas / Cloudstreet / Odette. Partnership with [Lunch Actually](https://www.lunchactually.com/sg/)'s 22-year SG dating reputation by hiring two of their senior curators.
- **Mumbai second (M5–M9):** 50 university ambassadors at IIM-A/B, ISB, Mumbai bar associations, founder communities.
- **Bangalore + Delhi + Jakarta + Hong Kong (M9–M18).** Each city gated by ≥2,000 verified members before the next opens.

CAC is the killer in dating (Bumble's post-IPO collapse was paid CAC ballooning). Lumin's flywheel is **referral + events + earned media**, not Meta ads.
