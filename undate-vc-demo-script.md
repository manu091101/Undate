## Read this first (2 min) — the strategic call

You asked for a demo that "showcases the functionality." For a pre-seed VC, a feature tour is the *weakest* version of this video. VCs at this stage fund **a problem worth solving, an insight others miss, and proof you can build.** So the script below uses the product as *evidence*, not as the subject. It spends the first 30 seconds on the problem and the wedge, then lets the live product carry the proof, then closes on why-now and the ask.

The good news after walking your whole app: **you have a real, working product, not a landing page.** That's a genuine pre-seed advantage. The live flow I verified end-to-end:

- **AI matchmaker onboarding** (`/onboarding/chat`) — a real, adaptive LLM interview. I answered one question; it followed up contextually and advanced a 7-step progress bar. It extracts OCEAN (Big Five), an MBTI read, attachment style, intentions, and conversation style.
- **The match reveal** (`/matches`) — "Reveal this week's match" → one curated person, chemistry %, personality tags, "Why your agents clicked," and an expandable **agent mock-date transcript** personalised to both people. Plus a live countdown to next Sunday's intro.
- **Admin curation desk** (`/admin`) — Approve waitlist → awaiting registration → Activate onboarded members. The human-in-the-loop your thesis promises.
- **Profile** (`/profile`) — exposes the actual compatibility model (attachment + Big Five + preferences).
- **Dashboard** — a live "agents in the ring" widget showing two agents negotiating a match.

That's enough to record a credible demo today.

## Before you record — fix these 5 things (the honest notes)

I'm flagging these because a sharp investor will, and you want to answer on your terms, not on camera.

1. **Your demo match shows 58% chemistry; your marketing claims 92%.** The live `/matches` card reveals Arjun at *58% chemistry · 58% algo fit*, but the homepage and dashboard both shout "92%." That inconsistency reads as either cherry-picked marketing or a broken algorithm. **Seed your demo account with a high-chemistry match (85%+)** before recording, or cut the on-screen number entirely and talk to the *reasoning* instead.

2. **"Hundreds of mock dates" vs. "ran 6."** Your thesis (and homepage) say the agent runs *hundreds* of mock dates. The live match says *"Your agent ran 6 mock dates and chose one."* Six is not a colosseum. Either bump the demo number or reframe the narration around *quality of simulation*, not volume — don't let the screen contradict your voiceover.

3. **MBTI is a credibility liability with technical VCs.** Myers-Briggs has weak test-retest reliability and is widely seen as pop-psych. Big Five (OCEAN) and attachment theory are scientifically defensible; MBTI is not. **Lead the narration with attachment + Big Five, mention the MBTI tag as a friendly "read," not as the science.** If your investor has a research background, leaning on INFJ/ENFJ will cost you.

4. **Your own /about page undercuts the core claim — decide how you'll handle it.** It says, honestly, that *"pre-meeting data explains almost none of long-term relationship satisfaction."* That's intellectually classy, but it invites the obvious question: *if pre-meeting data barely predicts outcomes, what is the agent simulation actually buying?* Your strongest answer is **"we don't predict love, we eliminate incompatibility and dramatically cut wasted dates"** — i.e. you're selling *filtering and time-saved*, not a love oracle. Make sure the narration claims the defensible thing.

5. **Have one number ready that isn't in the product.** Pre-seed demos die on "looks cool, but does anyone want it?" Be ready to say a real signal out loud over the closing frame — waitlist size, % who completed the voice onboarding, intro→date conversion, anything true. If you have nothing yet, say what you're instrumenting. Silence here is worse than a small number.

**One decision for you:** the script is written as pure voiceover. A 10–15s talking-head intro ("I'm [name], I started Undate because…") measurably lifts founder demos at pre-seed because investors back people. Recommend you film a short face intro and cut to screen. Marked optional below.

## The script + shot list

Narration is word-for-word (≈ the pace you'll actually speak). Screen direction is in **bold**. Practice the clicks once — the two "live" moments (reveal, agent chat) are the whole show.

### [0:00–0:20] — The problem (optional face-to-camera)
**Screen:** You on camera, OR the Undate homepage hero static.

> "Dating apps make money when you *don't* find someone. More swipes, more time, more ads. The result is a few hundred million people doing unpaid labour — sorting profiles — and mostly hating it. Undate is the opposite bet: do the sorting *for* you, and only ever show you one person."

### [0:20–0:35] — The wedge, in one line
**Screen:** Scroll the homepage slowly through "Four unhurried steps" and the "agentic matching ring" section.

> "Here's the mechanism. You never swipe. Instead, an AI agent that's learned who you are runs the dating *for* you in the background — and brings back one introduction a week. Let me show you the real product."

### [0:35–1:15] — Onboarding: the AI matchmaker (live)
**Screen:** Navigate to `/onboarding` → click **"Start the chat."** On `/onboarding/chat`, type a short answer and send. Let the next question render. (Pre-type your answer or paste it — don't fumble live.)

> "Onboarding isn't a form. It's a conversation. The matchmaker asks open questions — what a good weekend looks like, what pulls you toward someone — and from how you actually talk, it builds a structured profile: your Big Five traits, your attachment style, what you're really looking for. Watch — I answer naturally…"

**Screen:** Send the message; the AI responds contextually, progress bar advances.

> "…and it follows the thread, like a friend who's actually listening. No self-rating, no checkboxes. By the end it knows how you connect, not just what you filter for."

### [1:15–1:30] — The profile / the model
**Screen:** Navigate to `/profile`. Show the **Compatibility model** panel (attachment, Big Five) and Preferences.

> "That conversation becomes this: a private compatibility model — attachment, openness, conscientiousness, the things that actually correlate with whether two people last. Only your match ever sees your profile."

### [1:30–2:20] — The match reveal: the core moment (live)
**Screen:** Navigate to `/matches`. Pause on "One introduction. Considered." Then click **"Reveal this week's match."**

> "Now the part no other app does. Every week, your agent steps into a ring and runs mock dates against the pool — testing chemistry before any human spends a minute. Then it surfaces exactly one person."

**Screen:** Match card animates in. Point cursor at chemistry %, then "Why your agents clicked."

> "One introduction. Not a feed. And it tells you *why* — where you aligned, where the conversation flowed."

**Screen:** Click **"Read the agents' mock date"** to expand the transcript.

> "And you can read the actual conversation your two agents had — about you, in your words. This is the moat: we're not ranking faces, we're simulating compatibility and showing our work. Then the humans take over." **[Screen: hover "Start the conversation."]**

### [2:20–2:40] — Human-in-the-loop (the trust layer)
**Screen:** Navigate to `/admin` (Curation desk). Show "Awaiting review" and "Onboarded · awaiting activation."

> "And it's not a black box. Every introduction passes a human curator — approving members, activating profiles. AI does the heavy lifting; a person guards the taste. That's how the community stays small and trusted."

### [2:40–3:10] — Why now + traction + ask
**Screen:** Back to dashboard with the live "agents in the ring" widget, or homepage.

> "Why this works now: agents that can hold a real conversation and judge nuance only became good — and cheap — in the last year. The same shift that's automating support is about to automate the most human market there is. We're live, with [your real number: X on the waitlist / Y% completing onboarding]. We're raising [amount] to [hire / scale the matching ring / get to N members in city one]. If you've ever thought dating apps were fundamentally misaligned with their users — that's the company we're building."

**End card:** undate wordmark + your contact.

## Recording mechanics (quick)

- **Tools:** Loom or ScreenStudio (Mac) for screen+voice in one pass; or QuickTime screen record + a USB mic, narrate after. ScreenStudio auto-zooms on clicks, which makes the reveal moment pop.
- **Resolution:** record at 1280×720 or 1080p, hide bookmarks bar, use a clean Chrome profile (no personal tabs).
- **Pre-stage the data:** log in, pre-load `/matches` once so the reveal is instant, pre-write your onboarding answer. Do a dry run of the click path twice.
- **Two takes minimum:** one for timing, one for delivery. Keep total under 3:30 — investors stop watching.
